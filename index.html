How do I get the .eml file?
  • Gmail: open the email, click the three dots, then Download message.
  • Outlook on the web: open the email, click the three dots, then Save as or Download.
  • Outlook desktop: drag the email to your desktop. If it saves as .msg, use View source and paste it here instead.
  • Thunderbird or Apple Mail: File, then Save As (raw message source).
